Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

Introduction

Life Weeks Tracker is a simple client-side web app that helps you visualise and document your life in weeks. This privacy policy explains how we handle data.

Data Collected

This app stores user data locally in the browser (LocalStorage). We do not collect or transmit personal data to any server by default.

Export & Import

You can export your data as a JSON file and import it back. Exported files are controlled by you and not uploaded anywhere by the app.

Third-Party Services

The app may include analytics (e.g. Google Analytics) if enabled in the build. Consult the source code to see the configuration.
